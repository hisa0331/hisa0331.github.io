Research Interests

Passionate about advancing computational linguistics and optimization science to address complex real-world challenges.

Natural Language Processing (NLP)

Natural Language Processing represents a fascinating intersection of linguistics, computer science, and artificial intelligence. My research focuses on leveraging advanced NLP techniques to extract meaningful insights from textual data and enable machines to understand and generate human language with greater sophistication.

Key areas of interest include:

  • Semantic Analysis – Understanding the deeper meaning of text
  • Topic Modeling – Discovering latent topics and themes
  • Sentiment Analysis – Extracting emotional nuance and opinion
  • Machine Learning for Text – Developing robust neural architectures
  • Knowledge Extraction – Automatically identifying entities
  • Real-world Applications – Applying NLP to logistics and supply chain

Mathematical Optimization Problems

Mathematical optimization lies at the core of solving complex decision-making problems across industries. I am deeply interested in developing and applying sophisticated optimization algorithms to discover efficient solutions to combinatorial, linear, and nonlinear problems.

My research encompasses:

  • Linear Programming – Optimizing resource allocation
  • Network Optimization – Solving vehicle routing and facility location
  • Combinatorial Optimization – Tackling NP-hard problems
  • Reinforcement Learning for Optimization – Training AI agents
  • Multi-Objective Optimization – Balancing competing objectives
  • Supply Chain Optimization – Applying techniques to logistics systems

The Intersection of NLP & Optimization

The most exciting research opportunities emerge at the intersection of these two domains. By combining natural language processing with mathematical optimization, I aim to:

  • Extract insights from unstructured text data and convert them into structured decision-making problems
  • Analyze social discourse and sentiment to inform strategy
  • Develop intelligent systems that understand language requirements
  • Create knowledge management systems leveraging both semantic and computational efficiency
